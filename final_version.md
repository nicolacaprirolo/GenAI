Generative AI in Frontline Healthcare

About Me

My name is Nico and I am the founder and CEO of Holi Labs, a health tech startup focused on improving care delivery in emerging markets. My background sits at the intersection of technology and healthcare systems, and I am particularly interested in how AI can support clinicians working in resource-constrained environments.

The Problem Area I Care About

Frontline clinicians in public health systems across Latin America carry an enormous cognitive burden and patients more often than not arrive "too late" to be properly treated. A general practitioner in a Brazilian public hospital may see 40 or more patients in a single shift, each with different conditions, histories, and needs. The challenge is not that medical knowledge is unavailable, clinical guidelines exist, patient records exist, formulary data exists. The challenge is that managing health data and synthesizing all of it in real time, for every patient, is simply not humanly possible at the speed and volume these systems require. Generative AI is increasingly relevant here because the bottleneck is contextualization, pattern detection and presentation at the right moment, elements that AI excels at. I firmly believe that by creating a health information management system with multi-disciplinary prevention protocols at the top of the funnel can structurally decrease the cognitive burden and time it takes for a clinician to make a diagnosis and treatment plan, while at the same time increasing the quality of care by providing a second opinion supported by up to date clinical knowledge and evidence. As a consequence, my thesis is that we can leverage Generative AI to decrease hospital readmission rates by preventing illness before it arises for every person by proactively aligning lifestyle and eating habits regardless of socio-economic factors. 

Three Ways Generative AI Could Help

- Summarization at the point of care: condense a patient's relevant history, active medications, and recent results into a brief, structured summary a clinician can review in under a minute.
- Guideline-aware recommendations: surface treatment options that are consistent with current clinical evidence and with what is locally available, reducing the need for the clinician to cross-reference multiple sources during a time-pressured encounter.
- Clinical documentation support: assist with note-taking and coding after the encounter, reducing the administrative load that currently follows clinicians into their personal time.
